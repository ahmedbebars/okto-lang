# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in Okto a
harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, language,
or sexual identity and orientation.

Okto is bilingual by design. Questions and discussions in Arabic or in English are equally welcome.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, above all with people who are new to programming.
- Respecting different opinions and experiences.
- Giving and accepting useful criticism of the work, not of the person.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Behaviour that is not accepted:

- Sexual language or imagery, and unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone
represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through a
[private security report](https://github.com/ahmedbebars/okto-lang/security/advisories/new)
(it reaches only the maintainers), with "Code of Conduct" in the title. Every report is read and
handled fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that do not follow this code, and may ban, for a time or for good, anyone whose behaviour is harmful.
